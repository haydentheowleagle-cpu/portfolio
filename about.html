About Me

Empowering learners.
Designing with empathy.

Harmonizing the science of learning with the art of music.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Liberal Arts from Excelsior University and am pursuing a Master of Education in Instructional Design and Educational Technology at the American College of Education.

As founder of OwlEagle Studio, I design and deliver personalized virtual piano instruction for children, teens, and adults, including gifted, twice-exceptional, and neurodivergent learners. My experience includes developing curriculum in technique, repertoire, music theory, ear training, improvisation, composition, and performance, as well as using Canva, MuseScore, and digital learning tools to create engaging, accessible, and meaningful learning experiences.

Creativity, curiosity, and a learner-centered approach guide my work. As Vice President, Advocacy Chair, and a founding member of The Music Teacher Guild, I advocate for accessible, modern music education while collaborating with educators to advance innovation and equity in teaching and learning. These experiences continue to shape my growth as an instructional designer and educational leader.

A guiding idea

Meet learners where they are, then design a path that helps them go further.
